Why detection is needed

Rust risk grows quietly before it becomes visible damage.

Earlier classification supports safer maintenance

Rust weakens metal surfaces, shortens component life, and can become expensive when inspections depend only on manual review. Category detection gives teams a repeatable way to screen images and prioritize action.

Separating mild, moderate, and severe corrosion helps turn raw inspection photos into maintenance decisions. Mild findings can be monitored, moderate findings can be scheduled for repair, and severe findings can be escalated immediately.

Safety

Severe corrosion can reduce strength in vehicles, structures, pipelines, machinery, and storage assets.

Cost control

Early treatment is usually cheaper than replacement, downtime, or emergency repair after failure.

Consistency

A model-assisted workflow reduces variation between reviewers and keeps image evidence easy to compare.
